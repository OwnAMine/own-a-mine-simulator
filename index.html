⛏️ How To Play

Below are some tips on how to play Own A Mine Simulator effectively and become a master miner!

  1. Buy the default pickaxe and head over to your mine.
  2. Start digging until you find your first Copper.
  3. Mine the Copper and collect your resources.
  4. Head over to the Sell Shop and sell the Stones and Copper you have mined.
  5. Once you sell your resources, you should get at least $300. Use that money to purchase a better pickaxe.

If you follow these steps, you'll be on your way to climbing the leaderboard!

💰 Sell Your Resources

After collecting Stones, Copper and other resources, head over to the Sell Shop.

Selling your resources gives you money that can be used to upgrade your equipment and progress further.

⛏️ Upgrade Your Pickaxe

Once you've earned enough money, visit the Pickaxes area and purchase a better pickaxe.

Better pickaxes can help you mine resources more efficiently and progress through the game.
